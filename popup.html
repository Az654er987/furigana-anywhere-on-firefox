How it works

  1. Open any site with a video player and press play.
  2. If the player has Japanese captions, turn them on. Otherwise use Load sub above to add an .srt/.vtt file, or open Subs to pick a subtitle file the extension found on the page.
  3. The study captions appear over the video. Tap any word to look it up.
  4. Use Mine line above to turn the current line into a flashcard. Use Replay, Loop, and 0.5x-0.75x speed on the video to shadow a line.

Works on YouTube plus most HTML5 players (Video.js, Plyr, JW Player and plain <video> sites). Subtitles burned into the picture and DRM-locked players cannot be read; for those, download the .srt separately and use Load sub. Everything runs locally; nothing is uploaded.
