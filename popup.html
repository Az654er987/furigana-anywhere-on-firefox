Study Player

Furigana study layer for almost any web video. Open a video, press play, and load its Japanese subtitles.

Checking this tab

Saved words load from the video page.

How it works

  1. Open any site with a video player and press play.
  2. If the player has Japanese captions, turn them on. Otherwise use Load sub on the video to add an .srt/.vtt file, or open Subs to pick a subtitle file the extension found on the page.
  3. The study captions appear over the video. Tap any word to look it up.
  4. Use Replay, Loop, and 0.5x-0.75x speed to shadow a line.

Works on YouTube plus most HTML5 players (Video.js, Plyr, JW Player and plain <video> sites). Subtitles burned into the picture and DRM-locked players cannot be read; for those, download the .srt separately and use Load sub. Everything runs locally; nothing is uploaded.
